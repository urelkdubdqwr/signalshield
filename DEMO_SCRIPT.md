# SignalShield Demo Script — 3 minutes

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

“Most online opportunities ask us to trust first and verify later. SignalShield reverses that: before you trust the link, make it show its receipts.”

## 0:20–0:50 — The problem

“Financial and Web3 claims move faster than people can verify them. A confident post is not evidence. A single source is not consensus. And an AI score without receipts is just another thing to trust.”

## 0:50–1:35 — Product demo

Paste a claim and two public sources. Run the inspection.

Point out:
- risk signals
- source titles and URLs
- exact excerpts
- sourced versus unmatched evidence
- corroborated, single-source, conflicting, or unsupported result

Say:
“SignalShield does not hide uncertainty. It labels it.”

## 1:35–2:05 — Shareable report

Open the generated report link.

Say:
“This report can be shared with a teammate or community before anyone acts. The goal is not to replace judgment. The goal is to make judgment evidence-first.”

## 2:05–2:35 — MCP demo

Show the MCP server tools:
- `inspect_claim`
- `create_trust_report`

Say:
“SignalShield is also agent-native. Other assistants can call the same trust layer instead of inventing their own risk logic.”

## 2:35–3:00 — Close

“SignalShield is a small but important shift: from trust me bro to show me the receipts. When the evidence is strong, move faster. When it is missing or conflicting, pause before the irreversible click.”
